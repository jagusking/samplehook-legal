Does Suno Bridge work with Logic Pro?

Updated November 19, 2026

No, not yet.

Suno Bridge launched in beta on November 13, 2026. It works with Ableton Live 12 on a Mac only. Suno says it plans to add more DAWs and operating systems, but it hasn't named Logic Pro or given a date. Bridge also isn't a plugin, so there's nothing you can load in Logic in the meantime.

What Suno Bridge is

  • A standalone app, not an AU or VST. It runs alongside Ableton Live, and both apps need to be open.
  • It generates instrument and vocal parts for your session. Suno says they fit the tempo, key, style and groove of your Ableton project. You set the prompt window with Ableton's loop markers, and the result lands on the audio track you select.
  • It needs a Suno Premier subscription. The app is a free download, and each generation uses 10 Suno credits.
  • It uploads your project. When you open a session in Bridge, it uploads your Ableton Live project and its audio files to your Suno account so it can read the musical context.
  • Mac only for now. Suno's help center says it isn't available for Windows yet.

What Logic Pro users can do today

None of these is Bridge in Logic. They're the closest options, depending on what you need.

Suno Studio, then export to Logic

Studio is Suno's browser-based DAW. It doesn't sync with Logic, so set a manual BPM in Studio that matches your Logic project, then export WAV stems and drag them in. Studio can also make MIDI from a stem (10 credits), which you can open in Logic. MIDI made from audio is a transcription, so expect to fix some notes.

Logic's own Session Players

Logic Pro has built-in Session Players (drums, bass, keys and more) that follow your chord track. They write parts inside your project with no upload, but they don't sing.

Stable Audio's plugin

Stability AI makes a Stable Audio plugin that comes in an AU version for Logic Pro on the Mac. It does text-to-audio for sound effects, instruments and textures, with local models included and cloud generation through a Stable Audio account. Its guide doesn't list sung vocals.

A sung vocal hook: SampleHook on iPhone our app

If what you want from Bridge is a vocal (a hook, a topline, a chop), this is the workflow we built SampleHook for:

  1. Note your Logic project's key and tempo. Both show in Logic's control bar.
  2. Hum, sing or rap a rough idea into SampleHook, or type the lyrics. Pick a genre, voice and delivery, and set the key and BPM to match your project.
  3. Get an isolated vocal stem in about 90 seconds. Trim it on the phone. If a take comes back off your key or tempo, Conform to beat bakes the shift into a new WAV named with the key and BPM.
  4. AirDrop the WAV to your Mac and drag it onto an audio track in Logic.

Your beat never leaves Logic. SampleHook only sends your hum or your lyrics to make the vocal.

FAQ

Will Suno Bridge come to Logic Pro?

Maybe. Suno says it plans to expand Bridge to more DAWs and operating systems, but as of November 19, 2026 it hasn't named Logic Pro or given a timeline. We'll update this page when that changes.

Is Suno Bridge a plugin?

No. Suno calls it a standalone app that runs alongside Ableton Live. It isn't an AU or VST, so you can't insert it on a track in Logic or any other DAW.

Does Suno Bridge work on Windows?

Not yet. Suno's help center says Bridge isn't available for Windows right now and that it plans to expand to other platforms.

Does Suno Bridge upload my project?

Yes. When you open a session, Bridge uploads your Ableton Live project and its audio files to your Suno account. Suno says it only uploads the project you choose, and asks before including audio files it can't access on its own.

Can I use Suno Studio stems in Logic?

Yes. Export the stems as WAV and drag them into Logic. Studio doesn't sync with Logic, so set Studio's BPM to match your project first so the stems line up.

Is SampleHook a Logic plugin?

No. SampleHook is an iPhone app. You make the vocal on the phone and move the WAV into Logic. If you'd use a version that runs inside Logic, tell us at support@samplehook.app.
