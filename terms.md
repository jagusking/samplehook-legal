# SampleHook Terms of Use

**Last updated: May 18, 2026**

These terms cover your use of the SampleHook iOS app and the backend service it

## 1. What SampleHook is

SampleHook is an iOS app that generates short vocal samples from a guide
recording you provide. It uses third-party AI services (Suno, ElevenLabs,
Replicate) to produce those samples. You always sing or hum the guide; the AI
re-styles or replaces the vocal based on tags you select.

## 5. Credits

Generations consume credits. A Loose (style-led) generation costs **3
credits**; a Tight (voice-conversion) generation costs **1 credit**. Your
balance is displayed at the top of the recording screen. Free-tier accounts
receive 5 + 1 credits per calendar month, refreshed on the same day each
month.

Credits don't roll over. Unused credits at the end of a billing month are
forfeited. Top-up credit packs (consumable IAPs) do roll over until used.

If a generation fails (the AI provider errors mid-flight), credits are
automatically refunded.
