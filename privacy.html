Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

SampleHook (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps producers make short AI vocal samples from a guide recording or typed lyrics, using third-party AI services. It comes as an iPhone app and as a Mac app with an Audio Unit plug-in for Logic Pro. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what data we handle, why, and where it goes. It covers both.

We’ve kept this policy short and specific. If anything is unclear, email us at privacy@samplehook.app and we’ll answer in plain English.

1. Data we collect

We collect only what’s necessary to deliver the generation service:

DataPurposeWhere it lives
Device identifier — a random UUID the app creates the first time it runs. On Mac, the app and its Logic plug-in share one. Not your Apple ID, not your name, not your email. Tracks your credit balance, tier (free / Pro), and per-hour rate limit. Cloudflare. Deleting the app deletes the identifier, which unlinks the record (a reinstall starts a new one).
Guide audio — on iPhone, the short take you record before generating. On Mac, only when you use Hum mode: the bars of your Logic track that you choose to capture (up to 45 seconds). Uploaded to our backend so the AI can transcribe your words and follow your melody. Cloudflare R2, deleted automatically after 30 minutes. We do not keep guide recordings.
Typed lyrics, style tags, key and tempo — what you enter or pick before generating. On Mac, the key and tempo come from your Logic session. Tells the AI what to sing and how. Sent with the generation request; kept with the generation job record for 24 hours.
Generated audio — the AI-produced vocal. Delivered to your device for playback and use in your sampler or DAW. Hosted temporarily by the AI providers (roughly 1–72 hours). We do not re-host it. The final WAV is saved on your device: in the app’s Library on iPhone, and in your Music folder (Music/SampleHook) on Mac.
Transcribed lyrics — text extracted from your guide audio. Pre-fills the lyric-review step so you can edit before generating. Cloudflare KV for at most 30 minutes.
In-app purchase information — the signed StoreKit transaction for your subscription or credit pack (transaction ID, original transaction ID, product, dates). Validates your purchase with Apple’s signature, prevents replay, applies your tier. For a Pro subscription, the original transaction ID links the devices that use that subscription (up to 5) so they share one credit balance. Cloudflare. Subscription transaction IDs: 60 days. Credit-pack transaction IDs and the subscription’s shared credit record: kept for as long as the purchase can be presented again, so it can’t be credited twice.
Service logs — technical log lines our backend writes (for example a shortened device identifier, the product bought, whether a request succeeded). Finding and fixing problems. Cloudflare Workers Logs, a few days.

Audio in Logic stays on your Mac. The SampleHook plug-in listens to the audio you route through it to detect the key, and it does that on your Mac. That audio is never uploaded. The only audio that leaves your Mac is a hum you capture yourself in Hum mode and send for transcription and generation.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any Apple ID detail
  • Your location, contacts, photos, or any system permission beyond the microphone on iPhone (the Mac app doesn’t use the microphone)
  • Your Logic projects or beats
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) — we don’t run ads
  • Crash analytics from third parties — we use only Apple’s built-in crash reporting
  • Any behavioral or usage analytics

We don’t share your data with anyone outside of what’s required to produce the generation you requested. We don’t sell, rent, or trade any data.

4. Data retention summary

  • Guide audio uploads: 30 minutes
  • Transcriptions: 30 minutes
  • Generation job records (tags, lyrics, status, result links): 24 hours
  • Generated audio: not kept by us; available from the AI providers for roughly 1–72 hours; saved on your device
  • Device credit record: kept; deleting the app unlinks it from your device, or email us to delete it
  • Subscription transaction IDs: 60 days; credit-pack transaction IDs and a subscription’s shared credit record: kept so a purchase can’t be credited twice
  • Service logs: a few days

5. Your rights

Because we don’t store personally identifying information, there’s no account to delete and no profile to edit. To erase your SampleHook data, delete the app. On Mac, also delete the Music/SampleHook folder if you don’t want to keep your takes. Deleting the app removes its device identifier, which breaks the link to the Cloudflare credit record.

If you’d like us to purge a credit record sooner, email privacy@samplehook.app and we’ll work with you to find and delete it within 7 days.

6. Children

SampleHook is rated 12+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, email privacy@samplehook.app and we’ll delete the associated record.

7. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how data is handled, we’ll update this page and bump the “Last updated” date. We’ll also note material changes in the next app release notes.

8. Contact

Questions, concerns, deletion requests: privacy@samplehook.app
